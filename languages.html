Languages

The website in nine languages. Claude translates every line a visitor reads, a second Claude pass checks it, and anything you correct here stays as you wrote it. The catalogue — university and programme names — is never translated, and the office's screens stay in English.

The languages

A language that is on has its pages at /zh/…, /ar/… and so on, is offered in the header's picker, and is listed for Google. Switch one off and its addresses go to English.

Every line, in one language

Search the English or the translation. Edit a line and it is kept as you wrote it; "Again" asks Claude for a fresh translation; "Not a line" takes something that should never have been translated (a name, a code) out for good.

Blog posts

Every live post, translated whole in each language — title, excerpt, the Google lines and the article. Open one to read it or correct it; "Again" translates it afresh.
