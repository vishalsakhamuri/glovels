Common questions

The four things everyone asks first

Can I study abroad for free?DUMMY

In Germany, public universities charge no tuition — you pay only a semester contribution of roughly €150–350. You still need living costs and a blocked account, so "free" means free tuition, not a free degree.

What CGPA do I need for a public university in Germany?DUMMY

Most public universities look for 7.5 or above on 10. Between 7.0 and 7.5 a smaller set stays open, and below 7.0 a pathway or a private university is usually the realistic route.

How much does a study abroad consultant cost in India?DUMMY

Individual services start at a few hundred rupees; full end-to-end packages run from ₹9,999 to ₹74,999 depending on how many universities are covered and whether visa filing is included.

How long does a German student visa take?DUMMY

Six to twelve weeks from the appointment, and appointments themselves can take weeks to get. The APS certificate adds six to eight more, so start early.
