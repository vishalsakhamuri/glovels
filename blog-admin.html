Posts on the old Wix site

Paste the addresses of the posts to bring across, one per line (…wixstudio.com/glovels/post/…). Each is read, its pictures are copied onto this server, and it arrives here as a draft at the same address. Nothing goes on the site until you open it and press Publish. Leave the box empty to bring every post on the Wix blog.

Before go-live: test and off-topic posts

Two lists, worked out from the headline, the address and the word count of every post. Nothing changes until you tick posts and press Archive selected. An archived post comes off the site, the blog list, the sitemap and search, and its address sends visitors to the blog. Nothing is deleted: open an archived post from the Archived list and press Save to bring it back as a draft.

Tools

Upload .md — a markdown file opens as a new post with everything filled in; several files at once become drafts. At the top of the file you can put, between two --- lines: title:, slug:, tags:, excerpt:, cover:, author:, date:, meta_title:, meta_description:, keywords:. Without them, the first # heading becomes the title.

Make pictures fast — every picture on the blog gets small copies for phones (WebP, 480/960/1600 pixels) and a 1200×630 link-preview picture under 250 KB. The originals are kept. Run it in Chrome or Edge, and leave this page open until it says done. New pictures get their copies as soon as they are uploaded.

Tags — the list offered when writing a post, one per line. A post can have up to six; the first is its section. Each tag has its own page, /blog/tag/…, listed for Google once it has three posts.
